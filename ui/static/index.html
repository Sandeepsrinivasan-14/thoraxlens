    Operating points

    Thresholds were chosen on the validation set only, then applied to the test set. Precision (PPV) depends on prevalence: rare findings flag mostly false alarms.

    Subgroups

    Mean AUROC over 14 findings. AP films are mostly portable studies of sicker inpatients. Reasons for the age gap were not investigated.

    Models and how the ensemble is built

    Three networks carry weight; two older 224 px models were retired by the validation-fitted blend.

    What moved the score

    Test mean AUROC at each step. Resolution did most of the work; fitted weights added a small, reliable amount.

    Training curves

    Seed repeat

    The two 320 px models were retrained with another random seed on the same split. Run-to-run spread is about 0.001 to 0.003 per model and 0.0015 for the ensemble.

    Recipe

    Loss
    BCE with positive weights, capped at 50
    Optimiser
    AdamW, lr 1e-4, weight decay 1e-5, cosine
    Precision
    Mixed (fp16), micro-batch 16 × 2 accumulation
    EMA
    0.999 on the 320 px models; used for validation and saving
    Augmentation
    GPU affine: ±5°, 3% shift, 0.95–1.05 scale; no flips
    TTA
    Mode per model picked on validation (scale or flip+scale)
    Blend
    Greedy simplex search on validation, step 0.05
    Hardware
    One 4 GB laptop GPU

    Validation record

    Every choice was made on validation data; the test set was only scored. Checks ran after the models were frozen.

    Checks

      Gain over other systems

      Paired bootstrap on the same resampled patients, mean AUROC difference of the final ensemble. All intervals exclude zero.

      Known limits

      • Scores are not probabilities. Training weighted positives up to 50×, so raw scores sit far above prevalence (mean calibration error 0.30). Platt scaling fitted on validation brings it to 0.009 and leaves AUROC unchanged.
      • One hospital, no outside test. Generalisation to other scanners and populations is unknown.
      • Labels were mined from reports, not read from images, so some are wrong; networks can also learn shortcuts such as tubes and markers.
      • The test set was scored for several intermediate systems during development. No choice was made from those numbers, but it was not a strictly single test run.

      A research prototype that scores 14 thoracic findings on a frontal chest radiograph.

      Intended role

      Decision support research: worklist prioritisation or a second look, with a radiologist responsible for every read. Before any clinical use it needs external validation, a reader study, thresholds and calibration set for the target population, and the regulatory route for the intended market.
